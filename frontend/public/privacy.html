Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

CutList is run by [operator name], [address] ("we"). This page says what we keep about you and why. Write to [contact email] with any question or request.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a hashed password (we cannot read your password).
  • Your work: the 3D files, descriptions, sketch photos and voice recordings you give us, the project settings you choose, and the cutting plans and files made from them.
  • Saved offcuts, quotes and customer names you type into a plan.
  • Technical records: your address and the time of requests, kept in server logs to run and protect the service.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The site stores a sign-in token and your language choice in your browser; it sets no tracking cookies. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your address when a page loads.

Why we use it

To make your cutting plans, keep your projects, send you account emails (confirming your address and resetting your password), keep the service secure, and limit abuse.

AI features

When you use an AI feature (reading a description or a sketch photo, turning speech into text, the plan assistant, tips and explanations of problems with a model), the text, image or audio needed for that request is sent to our AI provider, Groq, which processes it and returns the answer. We send only what the feature needs. The software checks every number an AI answer uses against the plan. AI features are optional; the rest of CutList works without them.

Who else sees it

Our hosting provider stores the data for us. Our email provider delivers account emails. Our AI provider processes AI requests as above. We do not sell your data. If you create a share link for a quote, anyone with the link can see that quote, and only that; you can remove the link at any time.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. You can delete a project from your projects list, and you can delete your whole account (with every project, file, share link, API key and saved offcut) from the Account section. Deletion removes it from our systems; copies in routine backups are overwritten on the normal backup schedule.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Most of this you can do yourself in the app; for the rest, write to us and we will reply within a reasonable time.

Security

Connections are encrypted, passwords are hashed, and access to projects is limited to their owner. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you think your account was misused.

Children

CutList is for workshops and designers. It is not meant for children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app or by email before it applies.
